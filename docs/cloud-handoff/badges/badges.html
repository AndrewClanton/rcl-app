Royale Cinema Lounge · Badge proposal v3 · Oct 4, 2026

The Royale Badge Case

A plan to make badges a real part of the Royale: hundreds of them, earned by showing up, by the nights you came, by how long you've been with us, and handed out by staff for one-off events. Every badge is drawn by one system, so they all look like one family.

THE CATALOG

Badges become data

Today's 11 badges move out of the code into a catalog Back office can add to. Each badge has a name, a short line, a category, a rarity and a rule, or is handed out by staff.

THE ART · v2

Each badge its own thing

Enamel pins, stitched patches, ticket stubs, coins, ribbons and wax seals, each showing a real, specific picture: a moon over clock hands at twelve, a worn VHS tape, a crest. Loosely one family, never look-alikes.

NEW · TOKENS

A token of yourself

Every member gets a personal token drawn from their profile, and can give it to a friend they were here with. After a year, everyone has a shelf of keepsakes from nights at the Royale.

1 · Today

What's already there

11 visit badges

Welcome, Early Riser, Night Owl, Birthday Visit, 4, 13, 26 and 52 weeks in a row, and 10, 50 and 100 visits. They live as a list in lib/visits.ts, pay points once, and are stored per member in member_badges (unique per badge and period, so nothing pays twice).

Emoji for art

Each badge is an emoji (🎟️ 🦉 🍿 👑). Fine for 11, but it can't scale to hundreds: emoji look different on every device, and there aren't enough good ones.

No way to say "which showing"

Showings and house events have no series or tags, so "came to a Midweek Movie" can't be counted yet. That's the one data gap.

2 · The art · v2

Twenty badges, each its own thing

Version 1 was one shape with a glyph, and it looked repetitive. Here each badge is a real object with a specific picture you'd recognize across the room. They're held together loosely: the same hand, a short palette of Royale reds, golds, navies and creams, and a little highlight. Nothing else is shared.

New · Batches

Badges drafted from our event posts

Once a week, or on a button press, the Royale's own event posts are read and each one gets a draft badge: a name, a short line and art assembled from the parts library to match. Staff review the batch, change anything, and approve. An approved badge is tied to that event, so everyone who comes gets it automatically.

Where the posts come from

  • Our Facebook Page: read through Meta's Pages API with a one-time connection by a Page admin, using the Meta app the Royale already has. Reading your own Page's posts needs the Pages read permission; for a Page the app's admins manage, development mode should be enough, but that needs confirming with Meta.
  • Our own calendar: showtimes and house events already in the app, which are often the same events.
  • Paste a post: always works, no connection needed.

How a draft is made

Claude reads each post and suggests a name, a line, a form, pictures and a palette from the library, plus which event or showing it belongs to. Nothing is awarded until staff approve it. Posts that aren't events (a menu photo, a hiring post) are skipped.

New · One of one

Badges for one person

1 of 1 · for Tyler

The Usual Spot

Same seat. Same stories. Never a dull night.

Drawn from parts: a walnut pin, the bar stool, a brass name plate, speech bubbles.

How it works

  • An owner makes it in Back office (or asks Claude to draw one) and awards it to one member, with a private note if they like.
  • It's marked "1 of 1" and nobody else can earn it.
  • It shows on their shelf like any badge, and gets the full "New badge!" moment on the tablet the next time they check in.
  • Its pieces (the bar stool, the name plate) join the parts library for future badges.
New · Tokens

A token of yourself

Every member gets one personal token, drawn from their profile: their color, their initials (or photo), a pattern that's theirs alone, and a motif they pick. You give your token to a friend from your own account, with a short note if you like. A year in, people have a shelf of tokens from friends next to their event badges, little keepsakes of nights at the Royale.

The keepsake shelf

On My Account and the profile: tokens from friends and event badges together, newest first, each with who it's from and their note.

Finding a friend, privately

  • Here together: people who checked in the same nights as you, by first name and last initial. The main way.
  • Their link: paste a friend's profile link or handle.
  • Scan at the Royale: your token's code on your phone, scanned by theirs.

There's never a search of all members.

Giving gets noticed too

Your shelf shows "Tokens given: 14", and giving earns badges of its own: Generous (10 given), Social Butterfly (25 different friends). Each give plays a small moment on screen. Spam can't happen: one token per friend per month, nothing goes to someone who turned tokens off, and a declined token can't be resent that month.

6 · Tagging events

The lightest way to say "which showing"

A series on each showing

Add one optional series to showings and house events (Midweek Movies, Horror Month, Trivia, Members' Showing), picked from a short list when the showing is made. Duplicate and Repeat already copy a showing, so a whole run gets tagged at once.

Who "came"

A member came to a showing if their ticket for it was scanned at the door or bought on their account, or if they checked in that night while it was the only showing. Midnight and Matinee need no tag: they read the start time.

Backfill, once, with a dry run

A script counts first: Annual (old-site annual members plus Insiders+ annual), In It for the Long Haul (came from Indy), Owner. Then it awards for real. Past Midweek Movies count only if those showings get tagged first; otherwise the badge starts counting from now.

7 · Under the hood

Data changes

badge_defs (new table)

key, name, line, category, rarity, art (shape, glyph, color, or an uploaded image), rule and its blanks, points, hidden, active. Seeded with today's 11 under their current keys, so earned badges carry over untouched. Server-only, with grants.

member_badges (grows)

Gains the showing or event it was for and who awarded it, for staff awards. The "never twice" rule stays as it is.

Showings and events

One series column on showings and house events. Columns only, so no grants needed.

Badge art as parts

The parts library lives in the code (lib/badges/parts): forms, pictures, accents and name plates, each a small drawing function. A badge's art is stored as a short recipe: which form, which parts where, which palette, any plate text. A freehand badge can instead store its own drawing. One-of-one badges are badges with a single recipient.

Event post drafts

A badge_drafts table holds each post's draft (source link, suggested badge, matched event) until staff approve or skip it. The Facebook Page connection is stored like the other service keys. With grants.

token_gifts (new table)

Who gave, who got, the note, when, the month, and whether the recipient shows, hides or declined it. One per giver, friend and month, enforced by the database, never to yourself. Moves with a member merge and goes with an erase, like the other member tables. Server-only, with grants.

The token's look

A motif and a tokens-on/off switch on members. The color reuses their flair color; the pattern comes from their member id, so no two match.
